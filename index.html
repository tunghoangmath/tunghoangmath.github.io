About me

Hi, my name is Tung Hoang.

I am a PhD student in the Department of Mathematics at Tulane University. My research interests are in number theory and arithmetic geometry, especially elliptic curves, Selmer groups, and modular forms.

I am also interested in the philosophy of mathematics, particularly questions about mathematical discovery and understanding.
